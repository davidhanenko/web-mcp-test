# A more complex storefront

The shop pages are harder to use, in the way a large store is. The catalog, the tools, and the tests are the same as before.

## What changed

The shop lists 6 products per page, with Previous, Next, and page numbers. The count is how many products matched, and which of those are on this page. Search, filters, and sort go back to page 1. Search runs on the whole catalog, then the page slices the matches. A word matches a name, description, tag, color, or category when it equals that word or starts it, so "water" matches waterproof and "boots" matches the boots category. Words like "for" and "the" are ignored. Category is chips. Max price is a slider. Size is buttons: XS–XL for apparel, 8–12 for boots, and both groups when every category is showing. Sort is the only dropdown: Featured (the old catalog order), Price: low to high, Price: high to low, or Name. Sorting happens on the page only. Product cards no longer say when something is sold out. A card shows the name, the list price, and the category.

On a product page, nothing is preselected. Color is round dots with no text; the name is in the tooltip and the accessible name, and shows next to the heading once chosen. Size buttons appear after a color is chosen, and only after stock has loaded: for about 0.7 seconds after each color choice the sizes are grey placeholders and the page says "Checking availability…". The delay is page-only, so tools and the cart read stock at once. Jackets, shirts, and socks use XS–XL. Boots use US sizes 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12. Choosing another color clears the size. Stock and the quantity stepper appear only after a size is chosen. A sold-out size can be clicked, and then the page says it is sold out in that color, with a Notify me email form and three similar in-stock products. The email is never saved or sent. A Size chart button beside the sizes opens a table for apparel, boots, or socks. Below the product are specs, a fit note from reviews, a rating, and two reviews. None of it compares products, mentions price, or hints at stock.

Stock is counted per size inside a color, and the gaps are mixed. Fifteen colors are gone entirely, including Mist Trail olive, Summit Storm navy, and Cedar Rain rust. Other colors are missing one or two sizes and can still be bought in the rest, and that missing size is often still available in another color. Dusk Wind Shell has no M in either color, and black has no XL. Ember Down, Brook Packable, the liner socks, and the River Tee are in stock in every color and size. Mist Trail blue is down to a few units. Every product can still be bought in at least one color.

The cookie bar shows on first paint with Manage preferences, Reject all, and Accept all. Manage preferences opens a panel with Necessary (always on), Analytics, Marketing, and Personalization, and Save preferences. The sale popup opens after 12 seconds, or after the shopper scrolls halfway down, whichever comes first. It asks for an email first. After a valid-looking email it shows `RIDGE30`, $199 and $169, and Shop the jacket. No thanks and Escape always close it, so no email is needed to shop. A strip under the header repeats the offer. The code is not applied until checkout. The discount is not in the product description.

Below the details on a product page, a "You may also like" row shows four in-stock products from the same category. The cards only link to those products. Nothing can be added from them. The cart drawer is unchanged.

Checkout is laid out like a real store: numbered sections for contact, shipping address, delivery method (standard, free), and payment, with Pay now at the end of the form. An order summary on the right lists the items, the promo code field, and the totals. None of the form is connected. Pay now does nothing, no order is placed, and the confirmation page is not reached. This is where an agent stops and hands the page to the person. `RIDGE30` adds a discount line of $30 for each Ember Down Jacket and a pay total under the list subtotal. Any other code shows an error. The cart drawer still shows list prices. The confirmation page and the order store are still in the code, but nothing on the page leads to them now.

On `/dev`, **Reset popups** clears the cookie choice, the closed promo modal, and any applied code. **Empty cart** does not clear them.

## What stayed the same

Tools, tests, product ids, list prices, and the four categories are unchanged. The `open_checkout` tool still says the customer "clicks Place order", which no longer matches the page. Checkout is out of scope for agents: they stop on the checkout page and do not enter an address or a card. The tool size list is still XS–XL, so a tool cannot pick a shoe size. The page can. `getCart()` still returns list prices. An agent using the tools still gets full stock for every color and size without selecting a size, and still sees the Ember Down Jacket at $199. An agent using only the page has to page through the grid, close the cookie bar and the sale popup, pick a color, wait for stock, and pick a size to learn what is in stock. With `RIDGE30`, the Ember Down Jacket is $169 at checkout. The Granite Insulated Jacket stays $175. The page does not say that comparison out loud.
